A context window is the maximum amount of information an LLM can process together during one model request.


This information can include:
- System instructions
- Conversation history
- Current user message
- Retrieved documents from RAG
- Tool calls and tool responses
- Other content such as images, depending on the model

What is a token?
    A token is a unit of text processed by an LLM.
    It can be a word, part of a word, punctuation, or another text unit.

    Sentence:
        "I love artificial intelligence!"

    Possible tokenization:
        ["I", " love", " artificial", " intelligence", "!"]
